09 Ask, Act, Tell

Gartner describes three jobs for a well-built system. Ask, Act, Tell.

Ask is what we want to know. Act is the decision and the action. Tell is the report you hand to someone else.

A good dashboard does all three.

We add a fourth, Stop. When a number crosses a line the owner set, the system stops and a person checks.

10 One dashboard: Ask, Act, Tell, Stop

Here is what Ask, Act, Tell, Stop looks like on one screen, for one decision: the wing price. These are sample numbers.

Ask. Wing margin was 68 percent last month. This week it is 63 percent. The supplier raised the price of a 10 piece order from 3.90 to 4.40. That is 13 percent.

Act. The screen proposes one action. Raise wings from 12 dollars to 12.75, which brings margin back to 65 percent. The general manager approves or holds it, by Friday.

Tell. On Monday the owner gets one sentence, written from the same numbers. Wing cost rose 13 percent, we raised the price 75 cents, margin is back to 65 percent.

Stop. The owner sets two lines. If margin drops below 60 percent, or a price change is bigger than 10 percent, the screen stops and a person decides.

One decision. One screen. Every number on it traces back to the invoice and the point of sale.
